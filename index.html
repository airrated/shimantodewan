03 Perspective

I begin from due process, always.

1 Presumption of innocence - the burden rests with the prosecution, and it does not shift.

I am fascinated by the way law shapes society, and by how rigorous argument, evidence and reasoning can influence outcomes. At the heart of that interest is a belief in due process, and in the fundamental principle that a person must be presumed innocent until guilt is established.

04 Principal interest

Criminallaw.

My interest in criminal law stems from its intersection of justice, human behaviour and rigorous legal reasoning. I am drawn to the intellectual challenge of examining evidence, to understanding the psychology underlying criminal conduct, and to questions of accountability.

i - Justice

Justice

Not an abstraction. A standard that has to hold up in the particular case, on the particular facts, for the particular person in the dock.

ii - Evidence

Evidence

What can actually be proved, by what means, and to what standard. The most interesting part of a case is usually what the record will and will not support.

iii - Reasoning

Reasoning

Reading authority closely, testing an argument for where it breaks, and building the version of it that survives being taken apart.

iv - Accountability

Accountability

Why conduct is treated as blameworthy, how responsibility is attributed, and what a society is really deciding when it punishes.

v - Advocacy

Advocacy

The work of putting all of it in front of a court - clearly, honestly and under pressure. This is the part I am studying towards.

Reading and music

My reading tends to gravitate toward psychology, crime and science fiction - subjects that explore human behaviour, uncertainty and the limits of what we understand. Music is the other constant. Radiohead has a particular place in that world, especially Let Down, a song I return to often.

Training

I approach fitness through consistency rather than intensity. The gym is less about appearance alone and more about structure, resilience and control over the habits that shape everyday life. The science behind nutrition and performance interests me as much as the training does.

Cars and travel

A longstanding fascination with cars - specifications as much as design - and an equally strong curiosity about the world beyond home. Travelling, for me, is as much about understanding different places and cultures as it is about seeing them.

My ambitions extend beyond a career. I want to build a life abroad with the person I love - a lasting marriage, a close family, meaningful work, and the stability we have always wanted to create together.
